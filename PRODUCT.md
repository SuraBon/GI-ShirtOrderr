# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Branch supervisors (inputting employee shirt requisitions) and company administrators (managing dashboard, tracking withdrawals, and shipping items).

## Product Purpose
Manage and track shirt requisitions for Gold Integrate employees, check stock configurations, and streamline branch requests.

## Brand Personality
Professional corporate, reliable, efficient, clean, high-usability.

## Anti-references
Overly busy tables, SaaS cliché templates, unreadable gray-on-white text, or stretched input fields on mobile.

## Design Principles
1. Clarity Over Friction: Keep step-based wizard flows, checklists, and summary grids clean and predictable.
2. High-Fidelity Mobile Responsiveness: Focus on compact inline editing, proper tap targets, and eliminating page overflows on small screens.
3. Logical Data Representation: Make aggregate tables and stock badges ("พร้อมส่ง" vs "หมด - รอผลิต") scannable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA standards, high contrast text, standard input heights, and prefers-reduced-motion animation alternatives.
